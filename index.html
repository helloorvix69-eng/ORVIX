Our approach

From your idea
to the final product.

A straightforward process designed around communication and clear expectations.

01

Discovery

Understand your goals, audience, requirements and the problem to solve.

02

Planning

Define scope, features, deliverables, priorities and estimated timelines.

03

Design

Plan the layout, user experience and visual direction.

04

Development

Build the product using suitable technologies and agreed requirements.

05

Testing

Check functionality, responsiveness and important user journeys.

06

Client Review

Share progress and collect feedback within the agreed revision scope.

07

Delivery

Prepare the final files, handover details and launch assistance.

08

Support

Discuss maintenance and post-launch support according to the agreement.

Clear expectations

Professional work.
Transparent process.

Every project should start with a shared understanding of what will be built, when it will be delivered and what support is included.

◷

Project timelines

Timeline depends on the agreed scope, complexity, feedback and required integrations. A project schedule is confirmed before development.

▤

Clear deliverables

Project deliverables are documented before work begins.

  • Agreed pages and features
  • Responsive layout
  • Source files as agreed
  • Testing and handover
✎

Revision policy

Feedback and revision rounds are agreed in the project proposal. Additional scope or major changes may require a revised estimate.

◎

Communication

Project updates, feedback and important decisions are shared through the communication channel agreed with the client.

⌘

Testing & quality

We aim to review layouts, links, forms, navigation and supported device sizes before delivery.

♡

Post-launch support

Maintenance, updates and technical support can be discussed and agreed separately for each project.
